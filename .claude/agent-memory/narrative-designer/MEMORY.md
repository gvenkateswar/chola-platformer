# narrative-designer memory

## Voice rules (SCRIPT.md section 1, 2026-10-25)
1. He reports what is in front of him; no "epic," "legendary," "destiny."
2. He knows his station: a sevagan of fifteen years; rumor arrives as
   "they say"; nothing after 1025.
3. Short sentences, one breath each.
Two registers: Ilamaran (cards, hints, floats, ending body) and the record
(eyebrows, date lines, end-screen closing line, sources screen).

## Settled terms and spellings
Ilamaran; Sattan son of Kandan of Anaimangalam (invented benchmate, the
line of cost); Rajaraja and Rajendra ("the king," never "emperor");
Thanjavur; Nagapattinam; Anaimangalam; Srivijaya; Kadaram (Kedah, Merbok
river); kasu; sevagan; "soldier of the fleet," never "navy."
