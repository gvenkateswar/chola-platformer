Thanjavur

1025 CE · Kadaram has fallen

The Sea Belongsto the Tiger

Rajendra Chola's fleet crossed the Bay of Bengal and broke the power of Srivijaya — the only great overseas naval expedition in Indian history. Trade with Song China flowed free, and Tamil merchant guilds rose across Southeast Asia.
